bcollie-modbot Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how bcollie-modbot ("the App") processes data when it is installed on a subreddit through Reddit's Developer Platform.

The App is operated by the app owner and is used to assist with moderation and automated informational replies in Reddit communities.

Information the App Processes

The App may process the following Reddit data when triggered by subreddit activity:

  • Post titles, post bodies, post IDs, post URLs, and post metadata
  • Comment bodies, comment IDs, comment timestamps, and comment permalinks
  • Subreddit names and subreddit IDs
  • Reddit usernames associated with posts or comments
  • Moderator actions needed to reply, flair, report, remove, lock, or clean up content

External Fetching

The App may fetch image files from approved external domains in order to review image metadata for moderation purposes. This is limited to domains explicitly approved in the app configuration.

How the App Uses Data

The App uses data only to operate the moderation features it provides, including:

  • Detecting likely breed-identification posts
  • Responding to summon commands such as !appearance, !vet, and name commands
  • Flagging urgent rehoming and blocked crosspost cases for moderator review
  • Reviewing image metadata for moderation signals
  • Preventing duplicate bot actions
  • Deleting low-score bot comments according to subreddit settings

Stored Data

The App stores limited operational state in Reddit-hosted app storage, such as whether a post or comment has already been handled, whether a post was marked wrong, and IDs of bot comments created by the App.

This stored data is used only for app operation, idempotency, moderation workflows, and avoiding duplicate actions.

Data Sharing

The App does not sell personal data. The App does not use Reddit content for advertising or model training. Data is processed only as needed to operate the App's moderation and response features.

Data Retention

Operational data may be retained in app storage for as long as needed for deduplication, moderation state, and reliable app behavior, unless removed or overwritten by later app activity.

Third-Party Services

The App runs on Reddit's Developer Platform and relies on Reddit infrastructure for app execution and storage. If the App fetches external images, those requests are made only to the configured approved domains.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
